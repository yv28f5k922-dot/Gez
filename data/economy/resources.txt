# What the land produces. Each province gets:
#   a staple food (grain, rice or millet) grown by most peasants,
#   one trade good picked from the most specific matching rule below,
#   and fish if it is on the coast.
# Rule: scope [@terrain] | good weight, good weight...
# Scope is a province, state or region name, or "*" for everywhere.
# The most specific scope wins (province > state > region > *);
# at the same level a rule with a matching @terrain beats one without.
# Picks are deterministic (by province name), weighted.
