# Regiment unit types (Phase 6, owner's choice: 5 core unit types everywhere
# plus historical 1453 special units tied to estates, cultures, and local goods).
#
# key | Name | category | men | speed | attack | defence | morale | siege | cost (ducats) | upkeep (ducats/month) | goods | requires
#
# Categories: infantry, cavalry, ranged, artillery.
# Speed: 1.0 = normal foot pace (~30 km/day on plains); an army marches at the
#   pace of its slowest regiment.
# Goods: bought from the province's market when a professional regiment is
#   recruited (raising next month's demand there). Levies and mercenaries do
#   not buy market goods on recruitment.
# Requires (blank = every country):
#   estate:<key>              country has this estate (janissaries, mamluks, rajputs, tribes)
#   government:<g1>,<g2>      country has one of these governments
#   culture:<c1>,<c2>         country's capital or ruler has one of these cultures
#   region:<r1>,<r2>          country's capital lies in one of these regions
#   tags:<T1>,<T2>            country is one of these tags
# Multiple rules separated by ";" are OR-ed (any matching rule unlocks the unit).
